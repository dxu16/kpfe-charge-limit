Actual target and margin
------------------------
Software reported a4251mV normalized target. The PMIC programmed code130,
which decodes as4250mV normalized in the active5mV-step path. In2S mode that
same code represents8500mV total; in1S mode4250mV. The capture does not read
individual cell voltages or independently establish pack topology.

The configured/readback codes agreed in every sample. At the first target
change the hardware target was3.563mV above the reported gauge voltage.
This is NOT a measurement of comparator margin: the comparator and gauge can
have different sensing, accuracy and timing. It establishes that the firmware
places the voltage target extremely close to its contemporaneous gauge reading.

Quantization removed1mV from the requested4251mV target in this run. Thus an
assertion that quantization has NO effect would be wrong. However, the earlier
4255mV request is exactly representable. This capture does not prove rounding
is the complete cause or validate a replacement margin value.

Interpretation and next evidence needed
---------------------------------------
The new evidence localizes the fault to the delayed native voltage-target
transition with a very small voltage margin. The target was actually programmed
into hardware and the sampled protection configuration stayed unchanged.
There is no evidence here of a factor-of-two target conversion error or a
protection-register rewrite explaining this occurrence.

Insufficient comparator margin, differing voltage measurements, a transient
in the charger/power path, or an ordering requirement remain plausible. A
validated root correction needs the PMIH0108 OV/ECC specification and exact
interrupt mapping, or a faster synchronized event capture that resolves the
hardware fault source. Any proposed replacement margin must account for those
requirements; arbitrary5->10/50mV edits or disabling protection are not justified
by this test. A binary firmware modification has not been prepared or tried.
